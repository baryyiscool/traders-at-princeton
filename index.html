About

For students who want to trade.

Student-run and selective. We learn by practicing and competing.

Trading skills

We build the skills the job runs on: probability, expected value, mental math, and market making. The same work gets members ready for quant interviews.

Trading games

Members make markets and trade against each other in internal games and competitions.

Sponsors

Sponsor the club.

Sponsors meet students who spend their free time trading, through our resume book, talks, recruiting dinners, and trading games that carry their name.

Reach the right students

Members are current and past interns at Jane Street, Citadel, Citadel Securities, Hudson River Trading, D. E. Shaw, Optiver, Susquehanna, and IMC. They drill market making and compete in trading games.

Spend time with members

Give a talk, host a dinner or coffee chats, or put your name on a trading game and help design it.
