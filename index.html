    Make a market.

    This is the kind of question we drill in interview prep. Quote a bid and an ask around the answer. Tight and right scores best. Wide is safe but pays little. Wrong gets picked off.

    Question 1 of 6

    Loading question.

    Waiting on your market.Enter two numbers, with the bid below the ask.

    How it scores

    1. Your market must contain the fair value, or we trade against you at your price.
    2. If it does, you earn up to 100 points. The tighter the spread, the more you keep.
    3. If it doesn't, you lose points in proportion to how far off your quote was.
